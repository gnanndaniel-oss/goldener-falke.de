FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is there free parking?

Yes, Hotel Goldener Falke offers free hotel parking directly at the house for all guests (subject to availability).

When can I check in and check out?

Our regular check-in hours are Monday to Friday from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM. Beyond that, you can use our 24-hour check-in terminal (electronic) continuously from Monday to Sunday from 12:00 AM to 12:00 AM. Check-out is typically until 10:00 AM.

How does the 24-hour check-in terminal work?

If you arrive outside regular check-in hours, please inform us briefly by phone or email. We will activate the modern self-service terminal at the entrance for you. There you will quickly and easily receive your room key with your reservation name.

How far is the hotel from Augsburg University Hospital?

Hotel Goldener Falke is only about 10 minutes by car from Augsburg University Hospital. Josefinum can be reached in approximately 4 minutes.

Are there special rates for clinic visitors?

Yes, we offer special, discounted rates for clinic visitors and relatives of patients in Augsburg. Please contact us for your individual offer.

How far is it to Augsburg Trade Fair?

Via the B17, you can reach Augsburg Trade Fair quickly and directly by car in approximately 15 minutes.

Is WiFi free?

Yes, fast and free WiFi is available throughout the hotel and in all rooms.

Are there long-term accommodations available?

Yes, for longer stays of 1 week or more, we offer furnished apartments. These are more cost-effective and provide more space with self-catering options. Learn more about our apartments here.

Do you offer relocation services?

Yes, through our relocation service we support companies and professionals with moving to Augsburg with furnished temporary accommodations and personal assistance. More information about our relocation service.
