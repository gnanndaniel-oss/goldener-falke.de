Centrally Located for the Clinic

Hotel Near Augsburg University Clinic & Josefinum

Planning to visit relatives at the Augsburg University Clinic or looking for a room near the Josefinum? Hotel Goldener Falke in Augsburg-Oberhausen is the ideal choice: enjoy our quiet yet extremely convenient location, affordable rates, and maximum flexibility.

Augsburg University Clinic (approx. 10 min)

Via Bürgermeister-Ackermann-Straße and the B17, you reach the central clinic by car in no time. No tedious parking lot search at the hotel – you park your vehicle free of charge with us.

Josefinum Clinic (approx. 4 min)

The renowned Josefinum specialist clinic for children, adolescents, and women is also right in our neighborhood in Oberhausen – you can reach the hospital in just under 4 minutes by car.

Your Benefits as a Clinic Visitor

  • 24h Check-in Terminal: No matter how late it gets at the clinic, after a brief notification you can check in through our automated terminal completely independently. Simply give us a quick call.
  • Free Parking: Spare your nerves and your budget. Hotel parking in Augsburg is always free of charge with us.
  • Affordable Rates: We offer clinic visitors and patients budget-friendly rooms. Ask about special rates for longer stays.
  • Top Connectivity: Even with tram line 4 (direction P+R Augsburg West) you can reach us easily.
