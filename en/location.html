Location

Location & Directions – Hotel Goldener Falke

Our hotel is located in the Augsburg-Oberhausen district (Neuhäuserstraße 10, 86154 Augsburg). Thanks to optimal traffic connections, we are especially popular with business travelers, trade fair visitors, and car travelers.

Arrival by Car

The A8 motorway (Stuttgart-Munich) is nearby. It is best to exit the motorway at the Augsburg West exit and continue on the B17 towards Landsberg.
Take the Central Clinic exit, turn left toward Kriegshaber. Drive straight over Kobelweg to the brick church, turn left at the traffic light there, go under the railway underpass, and turn left again at the next traffic light onto Neuhäuserstraße. After about 50 meters you will find our hotel on the right side.
A free parking space is available in the courtyard.

Arrival by Train

Travel sustainably and comfortably by train. A major hub is Augsburg-Oberhausen Station ("Helmut-Haller-Platz"), which you can reach on foot.
If you arrive at Augsburg Central Station, simply take the regional train or tram (line 4, change to line 2) to Oberhausen Regional Station. In about 5 minutes walking, you'll be at the reception desk at the Goldener Falke.
