About Us

Hospitality with Tradition

For over 100 years, the name Goldener Falke in Augsburg has stood for warm hospitality and personal service. As a family-run hotel, we combine Bavarian tradition with the comfort you expect today.

Our hotel is centrally located in Augsburg-Oberhausen – with excellent connections to the University Hospital, Josefinum, and the trade fair center. In our guest lounge, coffee machines, computers, and of course our WiFi are available to you free of charge.

Thanks to our 24h check-in terminal, after a quick notification you can arrive flexibly outside regular check-in times – whether early in the morning or late at night.

Location & Directions

Centrally Located, Well Connected

Right in the heart of Augsburg-Oberhausen, just a short walk from the tram, bus and regional train. You can reach the historic old town in minutes.

University Hospital & Josefinum Just minutes away
B17 & A8 Direct access – trade fair in minutes
Public Transport Oberhausen railway station stop just around the corner
Detailed Directions

By Car:

Augsburg West highway exit → B17 towards Landsberg → Central clinic exit → turn left towards Kriegshaber → straight ahead (Kobelweg) until the brick church → traffic light turn left → under railway underpass → traffic light left into Neuhäuserstr. → after 50m turn right.

By Train:

Get a ticket to Augsburg-Oberhausen. Exit directly at Augsburg-Oberhausen station or change at the main station to R4/R6. Alternatively, take tram line 4 → transfer to line 2 towards P+R West → "Oberhausen Bahnhof/Helmut-Haller-Platz" stop.
