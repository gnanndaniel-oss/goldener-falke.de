About Us

Hospitality with Tradition — For Over 100 Years

The roots of Hotel Goldener Falke run deep into Augsburg's history. For over 100 years now, we have welcomed guests from all over the world in our house in Augsburg-Oberhausen. What began as a classic country inn has evolved over the decades into a modern yet family-run hotel.

Despite all modernizations – such as our new 24h check-in terminal or fast WiFi – our core philosophy has remained: genuine hospitality, personal service, and a family atmosphere where you immediately feel at home.

We are proud to run our hotel as a family business. From the warm welcome to the careful selection of our abundant breakfast buffet, you feel the personal touch. The well-being of our guests, whether business travelers, tourists, or clinic visitors, is our top priority.

"Arrive, feel at home, come again."
— Your Family Gnann
